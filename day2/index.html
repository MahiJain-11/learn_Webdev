🎀 THE GIRL BEHIND THE DREAM

She has big dreams, questionable decisions, 37 tabs open in her brain, and somehow still believes everything will work out. She wants to build a career. She wants to travel the world. She wants a beautiful life. She wants to make her parents proud. She wants independence. She wants money.

💭Becoming "HER"

Just becoming more you. Maybe becoming her isn't about changing everything overnight. Maybe it's waking up a little earlier. Saying no when you mean no. Taking your dreams seriously. Leaving things that don't feel right. Trying again after failing. And slowly becoming someone your younger self would be proud of.

🧠 THE THINGS WE DON'T SAY

Some thoughts never make it into conversations. They just stay somewhere between “I'm fine” and “I don't know.”

Someone got into the college they wanted. Someone got an internship. Someone is travelling. Someone already knows what they want to do with their life. And you're sitting there thinking— “What am I doing?” But life isn't a race with one finish line. Someone else's chapter 12 doesn't make your chapter 4 less important.
